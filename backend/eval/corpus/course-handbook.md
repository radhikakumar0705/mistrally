# CS101 course handbook

## Grading

Your final grade combines the weekly labs (30 percent), the midterm (30 percent) and the final exam, which is worth 40 percent. You must pass the final exam to pass the course.

## Late submissions

Late work loses 10 percent per day, and nothing is accepted more than 3 days after the deadline. Extensions are granted only with a note from student services.

## Office hours

Teaching assistants hold office hours on Thursdays from 3 to 5 pm in room 4B-217. Bring your laptop and the exact error message you are seeing.

## Final project

This year's final project is codenamed Project KESTREL: teams of three build a command-line weather dashboard in Go that reads data from a public API. The project is due on December 12.

## Lab machines

Log in to the lab machines with the shared account cs101-lab and your personal PIN. If you see error LAB-E73, your disk quota is exceeded; delete old build folders to free space.

## Academic integrity

You may discuss ideas with classmates, but all code you submit must be your own. Copying code from another student or from the internet without attribution is reported to the integrity office.
